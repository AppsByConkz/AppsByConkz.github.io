Kings Avoidance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

AppsByConkz respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Kings Avoidance mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Kings Avoidance does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

The game does not currently use an online account system, online database or developer-operated analytics service. Custom rules and game progress are not permanently stored by the game.

The application may temporarily retain game information while it is running in the background. This is not intended as permanent storage of personal information.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Kings Avoidance uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google provides advertising services that may collect and process certain information when advertisements are displayed or interacted with.

Information that may be processed includes:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Advertising interactions and app activity.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used to provide, measure and improve advertising, including personalised and non-personalised advertisements.

Where required, users will be asked to provide their consent or make advertising privacy choices before personalised advertising is served. Users may also be able to review or change their privacy choices through the options provided within the application.

Google and its advertising partners may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Kings Avoidance uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present advertising consent and privacy choices where required by applicable regulations.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, you may be asked whether you agree to personalised advertising or other uses of information for advertising purposes.

You can make your choices using the consent options presented within the application. Where available, you can revisit or change your privacy choices through the privacy options provided in the application.

Choosing not to consent to personalised advertising does not necessarily mean that you will not see advertisements. Non-personalised advertisements may still be displayed where permitted by your privacy choices and applicable requirements.

4. Data Storage and Security

AppsByConkz does not operate its own online server or database for storing Kings Avoidance player information.

The game does not require player accounts and does not permanently store custom rules or game progress. Game information may be retained temporarily while the application remains open or running in the background.

Google AdMob and its associated advertising services may process information as described in this Privacy Policy. That information is handled in accordance with Google's own privacy and security practices.

We do not sell players' personal information.

5. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

Kings Avoidance is a card-based drinking game intended exclusively for people who have reached the legal drinking age in their country or region.

The application is not intended for children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

AppsByConkz does not operate its own player database. However, third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob may process information when providing advertisements.

You can find information about Google's privacy practices and available privacy controls here: Google Privacy Policy .

For questions about this policy or privacy within Kings Avoidance, please contact AppsByConkz: appsbyconks.support@gmail.com .

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features or its services change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.
