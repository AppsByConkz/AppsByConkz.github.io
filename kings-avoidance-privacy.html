Kings Avoidance – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

AppsByConkz ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Kings Avoidance mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Kings Avoidance does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

The game does not currently use an online account system, online database or developer-operated analytics service. Custom rules and game progress are not permanently stored by the game.

The application may temporarily retain game information while it is running in the background. This is not intended as permanent storage of personal information.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Kings Avoidance may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising services may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Advertising interactions and app activity.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used to provide, measure and improve advertising, including personalised or non-personalised advertisements depending on applicable settings and consent.

Google operates its own services and handles information under its own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Cookies and Advertising Technologies

The application does not independently operate a website cookie system. However, third-party advertising technologies may use identifiers and similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

Where required, users will be given appropriate advertising privacy and consent choices.

4. Data Storage and Security

AppsByConkz does not currently operate its own server or online database for Kings Avoidance player information.

Information processed by third-party services, including Google AdMob, is handled under those providers' own policies and security practices.

5. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

Kings Avoidance is a card-based drinking game intended exclusively for people who have reached the legal drinking age in their country or region.

The application is not intended for children. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of certain information.

Where information is collected by a third-party provider, you may need to exercise your rights directly with that provider.

If you have questions about privacy in Kings Avoidance, you can contact us using the details below.
